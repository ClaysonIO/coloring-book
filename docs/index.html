A coloring book and sketchbook for every kid in the house

Start with a book of animals, download more than 190 pages from The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints right in the app, or draw your own pictures on blank pages. Each child gets their own copy of every page, so nobody colors over anyone else's work.

Download for Android Why I made it

Why I made it

My kids love the Church's coloring pages, and we used the official app for a long time. A few things kept getting in the way, so I built the app I wanted for our family.

🧽

No more clearing pages one by one

In the official app I had to clear each page by hand before someone else could use it. Here, every child has their own pages. When you do want a fresh start, you can reset one page, a whole book, or everything a child has colored.

🖍️

Their own pages, and no fights

My kids wanted pages that were theirs, without a sibling drawing over a picture they'd worked hard on. Each child picks their name when the app opens and sees only their own coloring. An optional PIN keeps little hands from resetting someone else's work.

🎨

Room to draw their own pictures

Coloring is great, but sometimes they want to draw something new. Every child has a sketchbook of blank pages for free drawing, and you can add new coloring pages from your own photos.

Who's coloring?

The app opens with a picture for each child. Tap your name and you'll see only your own books, pages and drawings. Add as many children as you like.

  • Coloring is saved separately for each child
  • Optional 4-digit PIN to stop accidental resets and deletes
  • Reset a single page, a book, or a whole account

Coloring that stays inside the lines

The line art always sits on top of the color, so colors never cover the lines and the eraser only removes color. Tap with the fill bucket to color a whole area at once.

  • Fill bucket, crayon, marker, paintbrush, pencil, highlighter, spray and eraser
  • A box of about 75 crayon colors
  • Undo and redo
  • Works in portrait and landscape on phones and tablets

A sketchbook for free drawing

Every child has their own sketchbook. Add as many blank pages as they want and draw anything with the same brushes and colors.

  • Add, rename and remove sketchbook pages
  • Turn your own photos into new coloring pages
  • Create your own books, or add a whole zip of them at once

More than 190 pages to download

The app comes with a book of animals. Tap More books in the app to download the coloring pages from the Coloring Pages collection on ChurchofJesusChrist.org, organized into six books: Family History, General, Christmas, Book of Mormon Stories, Pearl of Great Price Stories and General Conference. New books added to this site show up in the app automatically. See every book.
