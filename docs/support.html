Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub and include your device, its OS version and what happened. You can see existing reports on the issues page.

Common questions

How do I add a child?

On the "Who's coloring?" screen, tap Add, type a name and pick a color.

How do I clear a page?

Open the page and tap the reset button in the toolbar, or long-press the page in its book. To clear a whole book, use the reset button at the top of the book. To clear everything one child has colored, use Settings, or long-press their name on the "Who's coloring?" screen.

How do I stop my kids from erasing each other's work?

Each child already has their own copy of every page. To stop someone from resetting or deleting another child's work, open Settings and set a PIN for that child. With a PIN set, the app asks for it before deleting the profile or resetting a book or all of their coloring. Clearing a single page never asks for the PIN.

I forgot a PIN. What can I do?

The PIN is a simple kid lock and can't be recovered. Deleting the profile also asks for the PIN, so the only way to reset it is to uninstall and reinstall the app, which deletes everyone's coloring.

Can I switch between light and dark mode?

Yes. The app follows your device's setting. To change it, open Settings and pick Light, Dark or Automatic under Appearance.

How do I draw my own pictures?

Open My Sketchbook on the books screen. Tap New page to add a blank page.

How do I get more books?

On the Coloring Books screen, tap More books (the last tile, or the books button at the top). It lists every book on the books page of this website. Tap Download to add one. When a book is updated on the website, the app updates your copy by itself, and your coloring is kept. To remove a downloaded set, tap Remove on the same screen.

Can I add books from a zip file?

Yes. Tap More books, then Choose a zip file. Each folder of pictures in the zip becomes a book named after the folder, and pictures that aren't in a folder make a book named after the zip. The zips on the books page work too.

Can I add my own coloring pages?

Yes. Tap the pencil on the books screen to create a new book, or the pencil inside a book to add pages. You can pick several pictures at once from Photos or Files. Black line art on a white background works best.

Where did the coloring pages come from?

The animal pages were drawn for the app. The Church Coloring Pages you can download are from the Coloring Pages collection on ChurchofJesusChrist.org and are © Intellectual Reserve, Inc. This app is a personal project and isn't made or endorsed by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Does the app need the internet?

Only to see and download more books. Coloring and drawing work offline, and nothing you make leaves your device. See the privacy policy.

Where do I download it?

The latest Android version is on the GitHub releases page.
