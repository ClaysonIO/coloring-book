Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

The short version: Coloring Book doesn't collect, track, share or sell anything. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The only thing it does online is check this website for coloring books and download the ones you choose. Everything you create stays on your device.

This policy covers the Coloring Book app for Android, iPhone and iPad (the "app"). The app was made by a parent for their own kids and is offered for free.

Information we collect

None. The app never sends any information about you or your coloring off your device. We don't have servers that receive data from the app, and the app doesn't include any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools.

Downloading books

To show which coloring books are available and keep downloaded books up to date, the app reads the list of books from this website when it opens (and again when it's been in the background for a while), and downloads the books you choose. These are ordinary file downloads: the app doesn't send any names, identifiers or details about your device or your coloring. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as IP addresses, as described in the section on this website below.

What the app stores on your device

To work, the app saves a few things in its own private storage on your device:

WhatWhy
Profile names and colorsSo each child can pick their name on the "Who's coloring?" screen. A name can be anything, like a nickname or a first initial.
Optional 4-digit PINA simple lock that stops a child from resetting or deleting someone else's work. It's stored on the device as plain text and isn't meant to protect sensitive information.
Coloring and drawingsThe brush strokes and fills for each page, plus small preview pictures, so each child's work is there when they come back.
Books and pages you addIf you add your own pictures or a zip file, the app saves a copy of each picture.
Downloaded booksThe books you download from this website, and the list of books it last saw, so they work offline.

This information never leaves your device and we can't see it. Your device's own backup service (such as iCloud or Google backup) may include the app's data if you have backups turned on. That is controlled by your device settings and covered by Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

Photos

You can turn your own pictures into coloring pages. When you tap to add pages or a zip file, the app opens your device's photo or file picker, and the app can only see the files you choose. The app copies those pictures into its own storage. It doesn't read the rest of your photo library, and it never uploads your pictures anywhere. The app doesn't use the camera or microphone.

Children's privacy

The app is made for children to use with their families. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat, no links to social media and no way to share content from inside the app.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your device, so you're in control of it:

  • Reset a page, a book, or everything one child has colored from inside the app.
  • Delete a child's profile by long-pressing their name on the "Who's coloring?" screen.
  • Uninstall the app to delete all of its data from your device.

There are no accounts, so there's nothing to delete on our end.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't add any analytics, cookies or trackers to it. GitHub may log basic technical information, like IP addresses, when you visit, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Downloading the app from GitHub Releases is covered by the same statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Please open an issue on GitHub or see the support page.
